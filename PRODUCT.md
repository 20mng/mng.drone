# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS without framework or build step, deployed on Vercel from GitHub on every push. One serverless function (`api/whatsapp.js`) for the WhatsApp redirect.

## Users
Private individuals planning a wedding, celebration (proposals, communions, anniversaries, birthdays) or event, and small organisers/companies with presentations, openings or fairs. They arrive mostly from Instagram, WhatsApp links and Google, often on mobile, and want to see what the service offers and contact quickly.

## Product Purpose
Present MNG.DRONE's aerial filming service and turn visits into WhatsApp conversations. Success = a visitor fills in the short form (name, event type, date, place, what they want) and sends the WhatsApp message.

## Positioning
A single drone operator who plans the shots with the client, flies on the day coordinated with the photographer/videographer, and delivers edited pieces in both vertical (reels/stories) and horizontal formats within 3–5 working days. Tagline: "tu evento desde otra perspectiva".

## Operating Context
- Client finds the site via Instagram bio/stories, a shared link on WhatsApp, or search.
- Contact flow: form → opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message to the owner's number (number never shown on the page).
- Owner replies on WhatsApp and sends a custom quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Services: aerial recording HD/4K, editing and montage (vertical + horizontal), digital delivery optimised for social and commercial use.
- Delivery: 3–5 working days. Flights subject to current regulations and weather; booking subject to prior confirmation.
- Pricing is always "precio a consultar"; no prices on the site.
- The phone number must never appear in code or on the page.
- Do not mention "Andalucía" anywhere. Jaén appears only in structured data.
- Contact channels: WhatsApp form and Instagram @mng.drone only.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: MNG.DRONE (also written "MNG Drone"). Instagram: @mng.drone. Domain: mngdrone.es.
- Logo: wordmark "MNG" with "DRONE" inside the G (`assets/logoWhite.png`).
- Tagline: "tu evento desde otra perspectiva".
- Voice: first person singular, close and direct, Spanish from Spain, no hype.
- Dark mode is the main look; a light mode exists in navy and sky blue.
- Camera/flight vocabulary is part of the identity (REC, timecode, telemetry, altimeter, compass, RTH).

## Evidence on Hand
- Four of the owner's own drone clips (DJI, 4K 60 fps, 14–24 s): beaches and landscapes. No event/wedding footage yet.
- One low-resolution still of the drone in flight (`assets/drone.jpg`).
- No testimonials, client list, reviews or case studies yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
1. Every section should move the visitor toward the WhatsApp form.
2. Show real footage; never imply landscape clips are from a client's event.
3. Keep it honest and simple: no prices, no invented proof.
4. Mobile first: most visits come from a phone.
5. Respect what the owner explicitly kept; change only what is asked.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keep text readable in both themes; no horizontal scroll at ~390 px.
